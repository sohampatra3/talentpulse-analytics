## A useful investigation

Start with a role and a consistent exposure denominator. Compare treatment and control outcomes, then inspect device, market, acquisition, latency and application-error differences. An observed conversion advantage may reflect composition, missing tracking or chance. A causal rollout recommendation requires a valid assignment design and observation window. A job catalog alone cannot establish conversion or significance.

Adobe reports are additional observed evidence when an authorized report suite is connected. They are kept source-labelled and are not silently joined to a spreadsheet at an incompatible grain. The AI explanation can propose mechanisms and next tests; it cannot manufacture missing metrics.
