# Five-minute analyst demonstration

1. Open **Overview** and explain the primary application conversion definition. Change market and device filters; show that counts and charts are queried from Neon.
2. Open **Funnel & segments**. Find the largest transition loss, then compare device and user-type segments. State the number of sessions supporting each rate.
3. Open **Release impact**. Inspect the mobile CV release and its before/after error and completion changes. Treat the comparison as a hypothesis until a controlled test confirms the driver.
4. Open **Search experiments**. Compare manual search with the two simulated AI arms. Explain assignment grain, observation window, confidence intervals, corrected p-values, sample ratio and guardrails. Separate statistical significance from business rollout readiness.
5. Run a **live search comparison** for a Product Analyst role in Düsseldorf. Explain that jobs are synthetic records in Neon and that the provider reranks the same eligible shortlist. Show provider status and real latency; an unconfigured provider stays visibly unconfigured.
6. Ask **AI analyst** to explain the completion change and visualize affected segments. Inspect the evidence and chart draft before exporting a recommendation.
7. Show **Data & connections**: tracking definitions, quality checks, native Power BI assets, and the Adobe provider boundary.

Present the work as an independent product analytics lab. Do not describe simulated lifts as measured real-world model performance or claim this is an internal StepStone product.

Suggested closing: “I built a reproducible pipeline from behavioral events through SQL and Python statistics to a product decision. The key is consistent metric definitions, a valid comparison, and an honest explanation of what the evidence can and cannot establish.”
